    Settings and controls

    • Phone left thumb moves, right thumb aims. Push the stick all the way to sprint.
    • WASD move · Mouse aim · Arrows also aim
    • Left click fire (hold for auto) · Right click aim down sights on/off · Space jump, or vault through a window you are facing
    • Hold Space to bunny hop: you re-jump the instant you land and build speed. Strafe with A or D while turning the same way to gain more.
    • X inspects the weapon in your hands.
    • Safes and cash registers are locked. With a lock pick, press E and hit Space, E or click while the marker is in the green to set each pin. A miss drops a pin and can snap the pick.
    • Z dolphin dive in the direction you are moving. Time slows while you are in the air, but you aim and shoot at nearly full speed. A dive carries you through open windows. You land prone: crawl, press C to crouch, or Space to stand.
    • G fires the grapple gun if it is in your loadout. The hook flies out on a rope, catches walls, roof edges or the ground, and the reel hauls you in. Fire again, or press Space, to let go and keep your swing. Hook over a roof edge to climb onto the roof, where infected cannot reach you.
    • C crouch: slower and quieter, steadier aim, harder for infected to spot. Press it while sprinting to slide.
    • R reload · Q heal
    • There is no mouse cursor during a raid. The bag and loot window get a small gold in-game pointer.
    • Click anywhere on the game to lock the mouse. If it will not lock, the mouse still aims, and the screen edges keep turning you.
    • E search containers and bodies, open or close the loot window, drive · Shift sprint
    • Searching opens the container next to your backpack. Items take up grid space. Drag them across, press R to turn the one you are holding, or Shift-click to send one straight over. R or Shift+E takes everything that fits.
    • V switch view: shoulder, first person, classic overhead
    • 1-5 weapons · Tab bag · M mute
